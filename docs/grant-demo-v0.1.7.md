# SECOND MOTIVE — v0.1.7 Grant Demo Patch

This branch turns the existing v0.1.6 vertical slice into a first-play demo for the Draknek New Voices Puzzle Grant.

## Demo thesis

The first match should reliably produce this thought sequence:

1. We must save the CORE together.
2. My useful moves also advance a secret mission.
3. The AI is behaving oddly.
4. I can form a theory about its motive.
5. New evidence makes me reconsider.
6. The REVEAL makes an earlier move newly legible.

## Scope

- Grant Demo fixed opening: HUMAN = HUNTER, AI = BAIT.
- Deterministic spawn offset for the fixed demo.
- First-play intro with minimal rules.
- SHADE route previews on the board.
- AI-action readability and evidence cues.
- MID SUSPICION remembers the player's theory into FINAL READ.
- MOTIVE REVEAL explains BAIT evidence collected during the match.
- Random `PLAY ANOTHER MOTIVE` rematch after the fixed demo.

## Non-goals

No online play, story mode, LLM integration, account system, ranking, or large content expansion is part of v0.1.7.
